About CookBot

Smart Kitchen Support

CookBot helps turn ingredients into practical meal ideas in seconds.

Use the app to generate recipe suggestions, save favorites, keep a history of requests, and build simple weekly meal plans.

What You Can Do

Generate Create ingredient-based recipe ideas with optional country and diet filters.
Save Keep favorite recipes and rate them for quick access later.
Track Review your recent recipe requests from the History page.
Plan Build a simple weekly meal plan and grocery summary.

CookBot Focus

  • Fast recipe discovery from what you already have at home.
  • Simple filters for country-based and dietary-friendly ideas.
  • Lightweight tools that work without a complicated setup.

CookBot keeps your recipes, history, and weekly plans together.
